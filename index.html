The legend of La Fenice

More than a theatre

Teatro La Fenice is one of the most celebrated opera houses in the world. Its name means "The Phoenix", a powerful symbol of renewal and survival.

Throughout its history, the theatre faced destruction by fire more than once. Yet each time it rose again, preserving its place in the cultural life of Venice.

Three chapters

The story of rebirth

Three defining moments that shaped the identity of La Fenice.

CHAPTER 01

The Birth

Opened in 1792, La Fenice quickly became an important centre of opera and artistic life in Venice.

CHAPTER 02

The Fall

Fires destroyed the theatre in 1836 and again in 1996, threatening an important part of Venetian heritage.

CHAPTER 03

The Rebirth

Rebuilt and reopened in 2003, the theatre returned as a living symbol of resilience and cultural memory.
